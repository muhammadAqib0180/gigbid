# GA4 migration and tracking plan

**Client:** a e-commerce company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem

The client's the old analytics property was being switched off.

## What I did

I designed an event tracking plan, implemented it in the tag manager and rebuilt reports. The work used Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager and Looker Studio. Raw events are exported daily to the warehouse.

## Results

- Tracking plan of 42 events.
- Funnel reports live before the cut-over.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
