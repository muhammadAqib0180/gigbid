# Pricing elasticity analysis

**Client:** a consumer goods company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2024

## The problem

The client's prices had not changed in three years despite rising costs.

## What I did

I estimated elasticity per product line from promotions history. The work used Python, regression, statistics and Excel. 

## Results

- Recommended price change lifted margin by 3.2%.
- Volume loss stayed within forecast.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
