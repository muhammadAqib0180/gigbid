# Self-serve analytics with Metabase

**Client:** a B2B SaaS company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2025

## The problem

The client's every metric question went through one analyst.

## What I did

I set up curated models, saved questions and training for the team. The work used Metabase, PostgreSQL and SQL. Every service is containerised so it runs the same on a laptop and in production.

## Results

- Ad-hoc data requests down 45%.
- 40 active self-serve users.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
