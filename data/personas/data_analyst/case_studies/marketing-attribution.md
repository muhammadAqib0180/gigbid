# Marketing attribution analysis

**Client:** a e-commerce company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem

The client's the team credited every sale to the last click.

## What I did

I built position-based and data-driven attribution from event-level data. The work used Python, pandas, Google Analytics 4 and SQL. The data lands in a serverless cloud warehouse.

## Results

- Reallocated 18% of ad spend to higher-ROAS channels.
- ROAS up 0.4 points the next quarter.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
