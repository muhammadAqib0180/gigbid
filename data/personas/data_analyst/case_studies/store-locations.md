# Store location analysis

**Client:** a retail company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2021

## The problem

The client's a chain chose new sites by gut feeling.

## What I did

I combined demographics, footfall proxies and competitor locations into a site score. The work used Python, geospatial analysis, GeoPandas and Tableau. 

## Results

- Shortlisted 5 sites.
- 2 new stores opened above target.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
