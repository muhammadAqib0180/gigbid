# CRM data cleaning and de-duplication

**Client:** a nonprofit company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem

The client's donor records were duplicated across three systems.

## What I did

I standardised fields, matched records with fuzzy rules and documented merge decisions. The work used Python, pandas, data cleaning and fuzzy matching. 

## Results

- 48,000 records de-duplicated to 31,500.
- Mail-out costs reduced.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
