# A/B test analysis framework

**Client:** a marketplace company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2024

## The problem

The client's product teams declared winners after two days of data.

## What I did

I wrote a standard analysis notebook with guardrail metrics and sequential testing rules. The work used statistics, A/B testing, Python and SQL. Sample sizes are planned before any test starts.

## Results

- Standardised analysis for 40+ experiments.
- Cut false-positive launches.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
