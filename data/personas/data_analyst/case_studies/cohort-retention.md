# Cohort retention analysis

**Client:** a subscription app company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2022

## The problem

The client's the app grew sign-ups but revenue stayed flat.

## What I did

I built weekly cohorts and retention curves by acquisition channel. The work used SQL, cohort analysis, Looker Studio and data visualization. The data lands in a serverless cloud warehouse.

## Results

- Identified a week-2 drop-off.
- An onboarding change lifted day-30 retention by 4 points.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
