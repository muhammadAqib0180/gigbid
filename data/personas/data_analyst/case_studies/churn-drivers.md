# Churn driver analysis

**Client:** a telecom company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem

The client's leadership wanted to know why customers left.

## What I did

I joined usage, billing and support data and fitted an interpretable model. The work used SQL, Python, logistic regression and pandas. 

## Results

- 3 drivers explained 61% of churn.
- A billing fix reduced churn calls.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
