# Customer survey analysis

**Client:** a hospitality company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2022

## The problem

The client's a hotel chain collected surveys but never analysed the comments.

## What I did

I weighted responses, ran driver analysis and coded open text. The work used R, statistics, survey analysis and data visualization. Open-ended comments were coded into themes.

## Results

- NPS drivers identified across 9,000 responses.
- Two service changes prioritised.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
