# Inventory forecasting in Google Sheets

**Client:** a retail company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2022

## The problem

The client's a small retailer ran out of best-sellers every month.

## What I did

I built a reorder model with seasonality and lead times inside the sheet they already used. The work used Google Sheets, forecasting and Apps Script. 

## Results

- Stockouts down 30%.
- Owner reorders in 10 minutes a week.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
