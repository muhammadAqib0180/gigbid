# Excel reporting automation

**Client:** a logistics company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2021

## The problem

The client's a coordinator copied data between workbooks every Monday.

## What I did

I automated imports, cleaning and the summary report with one button. The work used Excel, VBA and Power Query. 

## Results

- Saved about 10 hours a week.
- Copy-paste errors eliminated.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
