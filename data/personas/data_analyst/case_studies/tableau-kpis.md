# Executive KPI dashboards

**Client:** a healthcare company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem

The client's executives received 20 static PDFs each month.

## What I did

I designed a KPI tree and interactive dashboards with consistent definitions. The work used Tableau, SQL and KPI reporting. Database policies ensure each user only ever sees their own rows.

## Results

- Used weekly by 60 managers.
- Monthly PDF pack retired.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
