# Sales performance dashboard

**Client:** a retail company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem

The client's regional managers waited a full day for the weekly sales pack.

## What I did

I modelled sales, targets and stores and built drill-down pages with row-level filters. The work used Power BI, SQL, DAX and Excel. The data is organised as one fact table with shared dimensions.

## Results

- Weekly reporting from 1 day to 15 minutes.
- Adopted by 35 store managers.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
