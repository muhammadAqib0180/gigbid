# Review sentiment analytics

**Client:** a hospitality company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2022

## The problem

The client's a hotel group could not see why ratings were slipping.

## What I did

I classified reviews by aspect and sentiment and built an interactive dashboard for managers. The work used NLP, Hugging Face Transformers, Python and Streamlit. 

## Results

- Classified 120,000 reviews across 40 hotels.
- Surfaced 5 recurring complaint themes.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
