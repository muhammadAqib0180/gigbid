# Dynamic pricing model for a travel site

**Client:** a travel company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem

The client's prices for add-ons were set once a year.

## What I did

I modelled demand elasticity per route and season and searched price points within business limits. The work used Python, scikit-learn, Bayesian optimisation and pandas. Changes went through a controlled split test before full rollout.

## Results

- Revenue per booking up 6.5%.
- No loss in conversion during the test.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
