# Two-sided services marketplace MVP

**Client:** a home services company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem

The client's a founder needed an MVP to test demand before raising money.

## What I did

I built search, booking, reviews and live chat between customers and providers. The work used React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB and REST APIs. Clients keep a persistent two-way connection for live updates.

## Results

- Launched in 9 weeks.
- 1,200 sign-ups in the first month.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
