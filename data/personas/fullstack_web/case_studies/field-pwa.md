# Offline-first PWA for field inspectors

**Client:** a construction company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem

The client's inspectors lost forms when sites had no signal.

## What I did

I built an installable app that stores inspections locally and syncs when back online. The work used React, PWA, IndexedDB and Firebase. A cache layer in the browser keeps the app usable without signal.

## Results

- Inspections captured offline at 40 remote sites.
- Paper forms retired.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
