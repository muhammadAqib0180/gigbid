# WordPress to Next.js migration

**Client:** a media company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2024

## The problem

The client's a publisher's WordPress site was slow and hard to change.

## What I did

I kept WordPress as the editor and moved the front end to Next.js with redirects for every old URL. The work used Next.js, WordPress, headless CMS and SEO. Pages are regenerated in the background on a timer instead of on every request.

## Results

- Page load from 4.2 s to 1.1 s.
- Organic traffic held steady through the migration.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
