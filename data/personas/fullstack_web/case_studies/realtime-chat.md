# Real-time support chat widget

**Client:** a B2B SaaS company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2024

## The problem

The client's the client paid for a third-party chat tool that did not fit their product.

## What I did

I built an embeddable widget, agent console and message persistence. The work used WebSockets, Node.js, React and Redis. Every service is containerised so it runs the same on a laptop and in production.

## Results

- Handled 3,000 concurrent connections in load tests.
- Saved $1,400 a month in licences.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
