# Conversion-focused landing pages

**Client:** a marketing agency company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2021

## The problem

The client's paid campaigns sent traffic to a generic homepage.

## What I did

I built fast, focused pages per campaign with variant testing. The work used HTML, Tailwind CSS, JavaScript and landing pages. Changes went through a controlled split test before full rollout. Every variant reports its funnel to the analytics tool.

## Results

- Sign-up rate from 2.1% to 3.4%.
- Pages load in under a second.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
