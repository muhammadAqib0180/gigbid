# Internal admin dashboard for a logistics firm

**Client:** a logistics company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2022

## The problem

The client's operations staff switched between four spreadsheets to find an order.

## What I did

I built a searchable admin with order timelines and bulk actions. The work used React, TypeScript, GraphQL and Apollo. Admins, dispatchers and drivers each see different actions.

## Results

- Replaced 4 spreadsheets.
- Order lookup from about 3 minutes to 10 seconds.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
