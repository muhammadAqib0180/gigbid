# SSO and OAuth integration

**Client:** a enterprise software company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2025

## The problem

The client's enterprise prospects required single sign-on before signing.

## What I did

I added SSO, just-in-time provisioning and audit logs. The work used OAuth 2.0, Next.js, Auth0 and TypeScript. Enterprise customers sign in through their own directory.

## Results

- Onboarded 3 enterprise customers.
- Security review passed first time.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
