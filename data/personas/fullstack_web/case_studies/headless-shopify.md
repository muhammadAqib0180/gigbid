# Headless Shopify storefront

**Client:** a e-commerce company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem

The client's the theme-based store was slow on mobile.

## What I did

I rebuilt the storefront on the Storefront API with server rendering and image optimisation. The work used Shopify, Next.js, GraphQL and Tailwind CSS. Every branch gets its own preview deployment.

## Results

- Lighthouse performance from 41 to 93.
- Mobile conversion up 11%.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
