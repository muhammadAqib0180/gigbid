# Habit-tracking app on Supabase

**Client:** a consumer company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2024

## The problem

The client's a solo founder wanted a production-ready app without managing servers.

## What I did

I built auth, streaks, reminders and an admin view on Supabase. The work used Supabase, Next.js, TypeScript and PostgreSQL. Database policies ensure each user only ever sees their own rows.

## Results

- 4.7-star rating.
- 2,800 weekly active users.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
