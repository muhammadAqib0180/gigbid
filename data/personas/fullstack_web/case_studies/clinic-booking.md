# Clinic appointment booking system

**Client:** a healthcare company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem

The client's patients booked by phone and many forgot appointments.

## What I did

I built online booking with calendar sync and SMS reminders. The work used NestJS, PostgreSQL, TypeScript and React. Reminders go out from scheduled jobs every morning. Users sign in through delegated access with their existing accounts.

## Results

- No-shows down 23%.
- Front-desk calls down 35%.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
