# Learning platform with video courses

**Client:** a education company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2022

## The problem

The client's a training company sold courses through a clunky plugin.

## What I did

I built course pages, progress tracking, quizzes and certificates. The work used Django, React, AWS S3 and PostgreSQL. Slow steps run on a background worker queue with automatic retries. Uploads are converted automatically into adaptive streams.

## Results

- 12,000 enrolled students.
- Course completion up 19%.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
