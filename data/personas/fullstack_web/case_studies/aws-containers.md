# Containerised deployment on AWS

**Client:** a B2B SaaS company (anonymised, synthetic)  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem

The client's deploys were manual and took most of an afternoon.

## What I did

I containerised the app, wrote infrastructure as code and automated blue-green deploys. The work used Docker, AWS ECS, Terraform and CI/CD. 

## Results

- Deploys from 45 minutes to 8 minutes.
- Zero-downtime releases.

## What I would do differently

I would agree the evaluation metric with the client in week one; most of the rework came from changing what "good" meant halfway through.
